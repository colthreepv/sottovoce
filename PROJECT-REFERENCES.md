# Project references

Short notes on apps worth learning from. Inspiration only; no implementation or design is assumed.

## Pronto — Windows dictation and meeting recorder

- Repository: [Jashith127/pronto](https://github.com/Jashith127/pronto)
- Windows app combining local Parakeet dictation with a meeting recorder for microphone and system audio.
- Product ideas to study: tray controls, a compact recording indicator, keeping stop responsive while transcription continues in the background, and a library with playback and transcripts.
- Relevance: a useful Windows-native reference for interaction patterns. Our core use is call recording and speaker-attributed transcripts, rather than system-wide dictation.
- Looked at: 2026-10-02.

## SottoVoce — live meeting subtitles

- Repository: [hameddavodi/sottovoce](https://github.com/hameddavodi/sottovoce)
- macOS app for live Italian-to-English meeting subtitles, with microphone or system-audio capture and a floating overlay; processing is on-device.
- Product ideas to study: choosing mic vs system audio, a movable/click-through live overlay, and showing fast provisional captions before refined corrections.
- Relevance: close in conversation/audio domain, though its live translation and Apple Silicon focus differ from our recording library and speaker-attributed transcripts.
- Looked at: 2026-10-02.
